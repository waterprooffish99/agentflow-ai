# Pilot Customer Onboarding SOP

Standard Operating Procedure for onboarding new pilot customers to the AgentFlow AI platform.

## Phase 1: Pre-Onboarding Preparation
1. **Vertical Identification**: Confirm the customer's business vertical (e.g., Medical Spa, Hair Salon, Consulting).
2. **Account Provisioning**:
    - Access the Super Admin dashboard.
    - Create a new Tenant record.
    - Provision the initial Business Admin user.
3. **Internal Documentation**:
    - Create a dedicated folder in the success repository for the customer.
    - Document any specific custom requirements (e.g., unique booking rules).

## Phase 2: Discovery Call & Information Gathering
1. **Business Profile**: Gather description, website, and brand tone.
2. **Services List**: Collect service names, durations, and pricing.
3. **Availability**: Confirm standard working hours and any upcoming exceptions.
4. **FAQ Set**: Identify the top 5-10 questions the AI should handle.

## Phase 3: Setup & Configuration (Walkthrough)
1. **Guided Onboarding**: 
    - Invite the customer to the onboarding link.
    - Assist in completing the Profile, Services, and Availability steps.
2. **AI Tuning**:
    - Configure the AI personality based on brand tone.
    - Test the AI in the "Sandbox" chat to ensure correct responses.
3. **Integration**:
    - Embed the chat widget on the customer's website.
    - Configure CRM webhooks if third-party integration is required.

## Phase 4: Validation & Launch
1. **Smoke Test**: 
    - Trigger a test conversation as a customer.
    - Verify lead capture and booking tool execution.
2. **Notification Check**:
    - Ensure the business receives email/SMS alerts for new bookings.
3. **Live Training**:
    - Walk the customer through the CRM dashboard.
    - Show them how to manage appointments and leads.

## Phase 5: Success & Optimization
1. **Weekly Health Check**:
    - Review the customer's analytics dashboard.
    - Identify any onboarding friction or AI confusion patterns.
2. **Feedback Loop**:
    - Collect feature requests or bug reports.
    - Deterministically categorize complaints into the Feedback Intelligence system.
3. **Upsell Discussion**:
    - If the customer reaches quota limits, discuss upgrading to the Growth or Enterprise tier.
